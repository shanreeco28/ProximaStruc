ProximaStruc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

ProximaStruc is a site monitoring and progress management workspace for construction projects. It is operated by [OPERATOR NAME] ("we"). This policy explains what personal data the ProximaStruc website and iOS app collect, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Data we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address and name that the provider shares. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email address.
  • Profile details you enter: job title or designation, contact phone number and birthday.
  • Project access: the projects you ask to join and the role an administrator assigns to you.
  • Site records you create: planning activities, inspections and comments, material records, diary entries, lifting permits and observations. Each record stores your email address and the time you created or changed it, as an audit trail.
  • Settings on your device: your theme choice and last selected project are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, health or payment data. ProximaStruc contains no advertising and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show project records to the members of each project, according to their role.
  • To keep an accountable record of who created, changed or deleted site records.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes it on our behalf. If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, that provider handles the sign-in under its own privacy policy. Members of a project you join can see your name, job title, contact details and the records you create in that project.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and profile until you delete your account. Site records you created stay with their project after you leave or delete your account, because they form the project's record of work and inspections. Those records keep your email address as their creator.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time. In the app or website, open Actions → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account and removes your profile and project access from every project. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke ProximaStruc's access to your Apple ID.

You can also ask us to delete your account, or to access or correct your data, by emailing [CONTACT EMAIL].

Children

ProximaStruc is a workplace tool for construction professionals and is not directed at children.
