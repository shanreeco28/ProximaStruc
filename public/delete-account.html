Delete your ProximaStruc account

This page explains how to delete your ProximaStruc account and the data linked to it. It applies to both the web app and the Android app.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to shanreecosocorro@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to ProximaStruc.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my ProximaStruc account.
  3. We confirm your request by replying to that address, then complete the deletion within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication record).
  • Your personal profile: name, job title, phone number and birthday.
  • Your project memberships and roles.

What we keep, and for how long

Project records you created (for example inspection requests, diary entries and material records) belong to the project's site record and audit trail. They stay with the project, together with the creator email shown on them, for as long as the project owner keeps the project. If you want your name or email removed from specific records, say so in your email and we will work with the project owner to do this where the law and the project's record-keeping duties allow.
