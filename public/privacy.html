Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data the ProximaStruc web app and Android app ("ProximaStruc", "we") collect, why, and the choices you have. ProximaStruc is a site monitoring and progress workspace for construction projects.

Who is responsible

ProximaStruc is operated by [OPERATOR NAME]. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

Data we collect

  • Account data: your email address, display name and sign-in method (Google sign-in or email), and whether your email is verified. Firebase Authentication by Google processes this to sign you in.
  • Profile data you enter: name, job title or role, contact phone number and birthday. All profile fields except name are optional.
  • Project records you create or change: schedule activities, inspection requests and comments, material and delivery records, site diary entries, lifting permits and personnel entries. Each record stores the creator's email and timestamps as an audit trail.
  • Project membership: which projects you can access and your role in each.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data. We do not use advertising, ad identifiers or third-party analytics, and we do not sell personal data.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To show project members who created, inspected or changed a record.
  • To control who can view and change each project.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and is encrypted in transit. Members of a project can see that project's records, including the names and emails attached to them. Project administrators manage membership. We share data with Google only as our hosting and authentication provider, or where the law requires it.

Retention

Account and profile data are kept while your account is active. Project records are kept for as long as the project owner keeps the project, because they form the project's site record and audit trail.

Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time. See Delete your ProximaStruc account for how, and for what is deleted and what is kept.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by contacting us at the address above. Singapore users have rights under the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Children

ProximaStruc is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes.
